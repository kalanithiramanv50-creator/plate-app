# Plate — calorie tracker

Log meals in seconds, track calories and macros, and see your progress. Works offline; your diary stays on your phone.

## Download (Android)

**[⬇ Download Plate for Android (APK)](https://github.com/kalanithiramanv50-creator/plate-app/releases/latest/download/Plate.apk)**

### How to install
1. Tap the download link above on your Android phone.
2. Open the downloaded **Plate.apk**.
3. If asked, allow your browser to *install unknown apps*.
4. If Google Play Protect warns that it doesn't recognise the app, tap **More details → Install anyway**.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer (64-bit phone). New versions install over the old one and keep your diary.

All versions: [Releases](https://github.com/kalanithiramanv50-creator/plate-app/releases)

## Food data
Packaged foods from Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org, Open Database License). Whole foods from USDA FoodData Central.
